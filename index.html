How to Use
Step 1 — Set up your items
Go to the Items tab. Your Manager.io inventory items are automatically loaded. Find the item (e.g. 60x60 Tiles) and click Set Dual Unit to configure:
• Primary unit: Box
• Secondary unit: Metre
• Ratio: 1.5 (meaning 1 Box = 1.5 Metres)
• Prices per unit (optional)
Step 2 — Convert quantities
Go to the Calculator tab. Select the item, then type a quantity in either unit — the other unit auto-fills instantly. Use the price summary to check totals.
Step 3 — Fill into invoice forms
Click any quantity or price field in the Manager.io invoice form above. The green dot will light up. Then click Fill as Box or Fill as Metre to insert the converted value directly into that field.
Step 4 — Invoice view
The Invoice View tab fetches the current invoice's line items and shows both unit quantities side by side for a quick overview.
⚠ Fill button note: The Fill feature works by sending a value to whichever input field you last clicked in Manager.io. Make sure you click the correct field first, then click Fill in this panel.
